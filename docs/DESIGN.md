# Design Reference

Visual direction for this portfolio, derived from the **Cohesion** Framer template
(https://cohesion.framer.ai). Layout, proportions, type scale and motion were
measured from the live site and implemented in local React/CSS. Project demo
content and gallery media mirror the supplied reference; they are not original
portfolio work. Cohesion is a commercial template by UIhub.design. Replace demo
content and confirm the applicable media/template permissions before publishing.

## Project detail pages

The six project routes share `ProjectCase`, its scoped CSS module, and small
client components for navigation/actions, the gallery and related projects.
The Reelio detail page is the layout reference:
https://cohesion.framer.ai/reelio-photography-film-studio-framer-template

- Desktop: three-image perspective entrance; sticky 1-column metadata alongside
  a 2-column article; two-column related projects with scroll reveals and hover.
- Tablet: centre-first sticky image stack; the article/sidebar remain side by side.
- Mobile: stacked article and metadata, repeated actions below the article and
  one-column related projects. Reduced motion disables image stacking/entrances.
- The six published projects are bambinoo, cafs, lms, oralguard-lk,
  ls-ecommerce and climedge. Each carries one thumbnail and three gallery
  images under `public/images/`; the demo galleries that shipped with the
  template (Vitalo, Artikle, Flowpath, Kudos, Reelio, Neozen) were retired
  with their assets.
- Visual geometry was checked at 390, 900 and 1280px widths. All six routes are
  statically generated by the existing dynamic route.

- **PT Serif** — *italic only*, as an accent inside a heading ("Hi, I'm *Name*!").

### Nav
Fixed to the **top**, not the bottom. Solid white bar at 24px radius with a soft
drop shadow. The active item is an orange pill at 96px radius that slides between
links (shared `layoutId`), driven by an IntersectionObserver band across the
middle of the viewport. A separate `Sign Up` pill sits 10px outside the bar.

### Hero (`#home`)
The section does **not** pin. It is a plain 100vh block that scrolls away with
the page; the only scroll-linked motion is per-element drift (see Motion §3).

- The wordmark marquee is **black, 240px, weight 900, running behind the
  portrait** — not a light strip at the top. This is the section's whole idea.
  The band is centred on the portrait, not on the viewport.
- Centred column: `Hi, I'm <serif-italic name>!`, then the role window, the
  portrait card, an overlapping avatar row with the client count, and the CTA.
- The role line is a **vertical roll** through a 32px window, not a cross-fade.
- Six 3D objects float around the centre column, three per side, each drifting
  on scroll at its own rate over a slow idle bob. They sit **in front of** the
  wordmark; only the portrait sits in front of them.

### About (`#about`)
Rebuilt from the Folira About-page composition. On desktop, five white
experience cards form the left column, a two-pixel vertical rail with black
milestone dots occupies the centre, and the right-hand summary sticks 120px
from the viewport top. The summary contains the two-line 60px heading, concise
portfolio introduction, the retained `Read My CV` link, and five workflow-tool
tiles. The white section uses 128px vertical and 64px horizontal padding;
subtle card borders and shadows preserve separation from the white canvas.

The whole composition is scroll-linked from `translateY(-200px) scale(.7)` and
opacity 0 to its settled state while the section top travels from the viewport
bottom to the viewport center. Experience cards then reveal individually from
`rotateX(-60deg)` and opacity 0 once each card is fully inside the viewport.
Each flip runs once and stays at full opacity, avoiding threshold flicker during
smooth scrolling. The sticky right summary fades upward by 40px, while its
heading words rise 10px with a short stagger. Reduced-motion users receive the
settled layout with no reveal transforms.

Below 1200px the gutters and type scale reduce. Below 810px, the summary moves
above the experience list, loses its sticky positioning, the timeline rail is
hidden, and the layout uses 20px gutters. The heading becomes 38px and workflow
tiles wrap when required.

### Testimonials
A 900px-tall slideshow, not a marquee. Each slide: 96px circular avatar, name
at 28/500, role at 14/500, quote at 20/400 over a max width of 940, all
centred, with three 10px indigo dots pinned near the bottom edge.

### Stack (`#stack`)
Twelve cards in a 1200px three-column grid, with 24px gaps and fixed 400px
heights. The 38px heading sits 192px below the section start; cards follow after
96px. Each face has a 48px radius, translucent lavender frame, 24px inset and
white inner panel. Logos are 120px vectors, followed by a 12px gap and 28px name.

Click/tap, Enter or Space flips independent faces around X, using 500px
perspective and the shared 500/60 hover spring. The back has left/top-aligned
20px text and the front fades as it rotates to -180deg. Cards reveal from
scale 0.8 / opacity 0 at a half-visible threshold and replay when re-entering.
The “Tap to Flip” cursor is outside transformed containers. A 720px turquoise
cube pins 180px from the viewport top behind the grid, with vertical floating
motion and an entrance scale.

Below 1200px: two columns, max-width 810px, 24px names, 18px descriptions and a
600px cube. Below 810px: one column, 16px gaps, 360px cards, 96px section padding,
48px heading gap, 20px names and 16px descriptions. Mobile hides the cube and
shows “Tap to flip” inside each front face. Below 360px cards gain 40px height to
keep the longest description readable. Reduced motion removes rotation,
scroll reveals and floating while preserving the toggle. Native buttons expose
pressed state and keyboard focus; hidden faces are excluded from accessibility.

The twelve `public/images/stack-*.svg` assets are the logo artwork displayed by
the supplied public reference, not newly designed marks. Their brands retain
ownership. Stack geometry was checked against the reference at desktop, tablet
and phone widths.

### Services (`#services`)
Two columns in a 1200px maximum width: 30/70 on desktop and 40/60 below
1200px. The left indicator sticks 96px from the viewport top. Its `01`–`12`
numeral is 200px/800 in `#F2F2F2`, clipped to a 146px window, with a 28px
title directly underneath (24px on tablet). Both update when the next service
crosses the viewport midpoint; scrolling backwards restores the earlier state.

The first eleven descriptions each occupy one viewport, with 24px/1.2 copy
146px down and 24px in. Each has a 240px-square 3D image, individually rotated,
centred 132px above the block bottom and floating 0–24px over a 2.8-second
linear, mirrored loop. The last service ends after its copy, without a shape.

Below 810px, the sidebar disappears and each article displays its own numeral
and 20px title, followed by 16px/1.2 copy with a 48px gap. The section uses
96px vertical and 16px horizontal padding. Blocks can grow beyond a viewport
on short or narrow screens to preserve space for the copy and artwork.
All twelve articles retain accessible headings; decorative images are hidden
from assistive technology and stop floating for reduced-motion preferences.
Desktop, tablet, 390px phone, and 320px compact layouts were checked against
the public reference. Navigation mirrors its viewport-dependent Services anchor.

### Pricing (`#pricing`)
Two plan cards side by side. Price is the accent colour per plan — orange for
Essential, indigo for Growth — and the CTA pill matches. Nine features each,
bulleted with a small dot in the same accent. Below: a `Need something more?`
strip with a large `Book a Call` pill.

### Projects (`#projects`)
2-column grid, all six shown. Card = tinted panel holding a 451:306 thumbnail
at 24px radius, then the category label (14/500) and title (28/500) *below*
the panel — indented 19px so they line up with the thumbnail rather than the
panel edge. A centred `View More` pill closes the section.

On pointer hover, project links show a cursor-centred `View Project` pill:
54px high, 20px regular Public Sans, white background, and a 54px black arrow
circle. The overlay is portalled to the document body so card reveal transforms
cannot offset or clip it. It ignores pointer events, hides on exit/scroll, and
also applies to Other Projects links. Reduced motion uses direct tracking.

### Contact / footer (`#contact`)
This one footer is Folira's, not Cohesion's (https://folira.framer.media),
measured at 1280px. Three rows on a 64px gap under 80px of top padding: a 500px
newsletter column opposite two link columns pinned to the right gutter; the
email address opposite four 44px social discs; then the wordmark.

The wordmark is the only part that needs script. Folira sets it in an SVG whose
viewBox is the text's own box, so it spans the full width whatever the string
is. Here a detached probe measures the run at 100px and the ratio becomes the
font-size — same result, and it re-fits when the box width or the webfont
changes. The observer keys on those two inputs only: re-fitting changes the
box's *height*, so observing that as well would loop.

## 3. Motion

The template's personality is almost entirely motion. Implemented with
`motion/react` plus CSS keyframes:

1. **Hero drift** — the hero does not pin. Every rate below was read off the
   live site by sampling `getComputedStyle().transform` under real wheel
   scroll at 0 / 200 / 400 / 600 / 800px. **Nothing moves for the first
   200px**; past that the offset is `rate x (scrollY - 200)`:

   | Element | Rate |
   |---|---|
   | Greeting + role window | -0.30 |
   | Badge + CTA | +0.10 |
   | Scroll ring (inside the card) | -0.40 |
   | Wordmark marquee | none |
   | Portrait card | none |
   | Solids, top row | -0.40 (Framer speed 140) |
   | Solids, middle row | -0.50 (speed 150) |
   | Solids, bottom row | -0.60 (speed 160) |

   The solids also revert to their appear state (scale 0.8) once they clear
   the 50% viewport threshold.

1a. **Role roll** — a single-line window with the list stepping up 32px at a
   time; the first item repeats at the end so the wrap rolls the same
   direction. The window needs `contain: paint`, or the compositor leaks the
   outgoing line past the clip mid-transition. The live site leaks the same
   way, so a second line showing mid-roll is correct, not a bug.
2. **Experience timeline** — About uses a reversible scroll-linked wrapper
   entrance, one-time 3D card flips, a sticky summary fade, and a word-staggered
   heading reveal.
3. **Infinite marquees** — hero wordmark and testimonials.
   `marquee-track` utility: content rendered twice, track translated -50%.
4. **Card flip** — the portrait rests flat and turns -180 on hover, spring
   500/60 over `perspective: 500`. No resting tilt: on the reference both
   faces read as axis-aligned rectangles.
5. **Pill CTA hover** — padding-right 48 -> 99, so the pill grows 51px to the
   right; the dark circle sits just outside the right edge and is revealed by
   the pill's own `overflow: hidden` as it grows. No letter-spacing change and
   no rotation on the circle — the live button animates padding only.
6. **Sticky service indicator** — CSS sticky positioning with viewport-midpoint
   number/title updates and independent, mirrored 3D-shape float loops.
7. **Sliding nav pill** — shared `layoutId` with a spring transition.

8. **Hero particle field** — a WebGL layer behind the solids
   ([`ParticleField`](../frontend/src/components/effects/ParticleField.tsx)), recreating the radial
   dash background from https://antigravity.google. Not part of the Cohesion
   reference; see section 3a.
9. **Video wall** — the scroll-in video panel below About
   (`VideoWall`), also from Antigravity rather
   than Cohesion; see section 3b. The panel is not in the current build — no
   component or route renders it.
10. **Get in touch reveal** — the contact block below Projects
    ([`GetInTouch`](../frontend/src/components/home/GetInTouch.tsx)) opens through an expanding
    elliptical mask while content scales 0.8 → 1 and fades in. Both follow
    scroll position and reverse on the way out; see section 3c.

All of it is disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.

### 3a. Hero particle field

Thousands of tiny coloured dashes, Poisson-scattered and lying along the radius
of a slow drifting ring. It is a recreation of the Antigravity hero background,
built from that page's published behaviour and re-derived in local code — no
library, no vendored source. Ours renders on **raw WebGL, one `gl.POINTS` draw
call**, where the reference uses three.js with a GPGPU ping-pong framebuffer.

The reference stores each particle's *output* position and feeds it back with
`pos = 0.8·prevOut + push`, `out = ref + disp + 0.25·pos`, which settles at
`out = 1.25·(ref + disp) + 0.3125·push`. Solving that steady state in closed
form removes the simulation entirely: position, scale, dash angle and colour
are pure functions of the particle's rest position and the clock, so motion is
frame-rate independent and there is no float-texture requirement. The velocity
trail (`v = 0.5v + 0.25s`) collapses to `0.5·s` the same way.

Parameters read off the live hero: density 230 (Poisson spacing
3.87–4.87 / 250), ring radius `0.175 + 0.03·sin t + 0.02·cos 3t`, ring widths
0.006 / 0.107, displacement 0.62, particle scale 0.59. Ours keeps all of them;
only the colours (portfolio indigo / accent / ink instead of blue / red /
yellow) and the dash size differ, and both are props.

Two deliberate departures:

- **Framing.** The reference frames purely by height, which on a portrait phone
  puts the whole ring outside the viewport. Below 1:1 ours frames by width
  instead, so the ring still reads on mobile.
- **Idle scale.** Point size tracks viewport width as the reference does, but
  with a floor at 720px — the reference's phone hero is close to invisible.

The canvas pauses off-screen (IntersectionObserver) and on hidden tabs, redraws
one still frame under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, and tracks
`devicePixelRatio` changes so browser zoom does not leave it blurry.

### 3b. Video wall

The panel between About and Stack, recreating Antigravity's
`[data-youtube-video-section]`. That section is driven by GSAP:

```js
gsap.from(section, {
  scrollTrigger: { trigger: section, start: "top bottom",
                   end: "top center", scrub: 1 },
  scale: 0.5, ease: "power2.out",
})
```

so the panel opens from half size as its top travels from the viewport bottom
to the viewport centre, and a second ScrollTrigger spanning `top bottom` →
`bottom top` plays the muted video on enter and pauses it on leave.

Ours uses the tools already in the project instead of adding GSAP:
`useScroll({ offset: ["start end", "start center"] })` is the same window,
`useSpring` with [`scrubSpring`](../frontend/src/lib/motion.ts) stands in for `scrub: 1`
(a one-second catch-up, not a hard lock to the scrollbar), GSAP's `power2` is
the cubic curve `1 - (1 - t)³`, and an IntersectionObserver at threshold 0 is
the same viewport overlap. Verified against the live boundaries: with the
section top exactly at the viewport bottom the panel measures `scale(0.5)` and
the video is paused; scrolling it into view runs 0.5 → 1 and starts playback.

The panel keeps the reference's negative inline margin, which bleeds it back
over the page gutter to sit 6px off the screen edge on narrow viewports. Corner
radius is ours (`--radius-panel`, 48px) rather than the reference's 24px, to
match the portrait card and the Contact block.

Deliberately dropped: the reference's play-button cursor and YouTube modal. Our
panel only loops, so there is nothing to click — the video carries
`pointer-events: none` unless controls are needed.

For playback smoothness the clip is preloaded in full (`preload="auto"`; it is
711KB / 2.75s), the box reserves its 16:9 space from the native 1920×1080 so
nothing shifts on load, and the wrapper is black so there is no flash before
the first frame paints. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the panel does
not scale, does not autoplay, and shows native controls instead — as it also
does if a browser refuses the muted autoplay.

### 3c. Get in touch

The contact section between Projects and the footer follows the contact block
on [Folira's About page](https://folira.framer.media/about). Existing portfolio
copy, service options, portrait and contact email are retained.

Desktop: a 1500px-max container, 54px left / 64px right gutters, and 128px
vertical padding on a black band over #fff. The reveal's surrounding surface
matches the preceding Projects section, avoiding a horizontal white/gray seam.
Folira uses #f4f4f4 for both adjacent sections; this portfolio uses white for both.
A 32%-wide sidebar contains the
60px Geist heading, intro, 4:3 portrait, and message/call tabs. The form is 49%
wide: Name/Email and Company/Stage are paired; Service and Notes span the form;
Budget/Timeline share a row. Controls are 48px tall, Notes 140px, with 12px
radii, 24px between field groups and 40px before the submit button.

Below 1200px: 100px vertical padding, 40px side gutters, 48px heading, a 57%
form, and stacked identity fields. Below 810px: 20px gutters, 34px heading,
hidden portrait, header/tabs above a single-column form with a 64px gap.
Narrow sidebars stack the two tabs instead of clipping their labels.

The reference's entrance is scroll-linked, not a one-time appear spring:
an elliptical mask expands from 100px to 250% of the band's dimensions as
the section travels from entering to fully crossing the viewport bottom.
Over the first 55% of that progress, content fades 0–1 and scales 0.8–1.
Scrolling backwards reverses it. ResizeObserver keeps the mask responsive.
Mask, opacity and scale share the JS scroll clock via function transforms.
Do not replace the content transforms with range-based transforms: Motion 13
can promote opacity to a native ViewTimeline whose progress diverges from the
mask, leaving a fully expanded black band with invisible content on fresh loads.
Reduced motion and keyboard focus reveal content without the masking effect.

Message/call tabs animate their widths, active colors and labels; panels
crossfade/slide. Arrow keys and Home/End switch tabs, and form values persist
between panels. The navigation Contact link now targets this section.

The form currently prepares a mailto draft with its validated fields; it does
not claim server delivery. The call panel provides an email fallback until the
owner supplies a real Cal.com or Calendly booking URL. No template demo calendar
is connected, and no bookings or test inquiries were sent.

**Scroll container gotcha:** `overflow-x: hidden` on `<body>` or `<html>` makes
that element the scroll container and silently breaks every scroll-linked
animation. `globals.css` uses `overflow-x: clip` on `<html>` instead.
